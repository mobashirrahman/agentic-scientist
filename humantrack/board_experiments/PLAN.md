# Local T1, heart and embryo ablations

Prediction seeds are 42, 101 and 211. Spatial scoring seeds are 301, 302 and 303. All predictions use 3,000 cells for matched comparisons. T1 uses the released three-input E12.5 configuration and reports composition veto outcomes. An independent calibrated selector is still required for promotion. Heart compares anchor, geometry, local expression, their combination and Codex at matched cell count. Embryo compares endpoint mixture, an early scaffold with region shifts and the same scaffold with spatial donor assignment.

The two public E8.5 heart guard views and the public E8.0 and E7.25 embryo checks are diagnostic. Promotion still requires preregistered calibrated checks and comparison at the scored baseline settings. No private data or portal access is needed for this experiment.

The exploratory T1 chamber assay forecasts all four programs to E14.5 at seed 42. It uses only wild-type E14.5 annotations from the Feng atlas, weights its four chambers equally within each strain, and measures L1 distance among normalized cardiomyocyte, endothelial and epicardial fractions. Blood is covered by the separate E12.5 veto. The historical baseline must be closer than Enigma in both strains. This is a cross-assay diagnostic even if it reproduces the order.

The Codex heart reference uses its native output count because that program fixes it internally. The three heart ablations and first-bundle anchor are matched at 3,000 cells.

After the original embryo ablation lost on both public targets, a follow-up tests a smaller geometry change. It preserves the original endpoint mixture, cell positions within each type, and cell-to-expression pairing. Type centroids move toward their interpolated position by at most five percent of the robust axis extent before interpolation weighting. The same splits and seeds apply. This follow-up is exploratory because the earlier results informed its design.
