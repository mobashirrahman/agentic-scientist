# Start with bracket stages and annotations

For Task 3, the embryo setting and Task 1, the external resources that can move our scores are narrower than the public atlas list suggests, and the first lever differs by task. **Task 3 has no usable beta-catenin response data**, because every Ctnnb1 or Wnt-loss measurement is held out, so the gain must come from correct-sign priors built from canonical Wnt annotation and from response magnitudes calibrated on non-Wnt Mesp1-lineage knockouts. **The embryo interpolation depends on its two bracket stages**, E7.25 and E8.0, so the Pijuan-Sala timepoints at those ages, the Harland E7.25 spatial set and an E8.0-only three-dimensional subset from a 2025 Cell digital-embryo paper form the core references. **Task 1 has one cleared whole-transcriptome heart reference now**: the ENCODE E14.5 heart polyA RNA-seq experiment, allowed with disclosure. Bulk and annotation resources are not single-cell data, so the data-page sentence does not clearly block them, but the rules still apply and the organisers should confirm. Start with the Task 1 bulk reference and the Task 3 annotation layer, and send one organiser message today that covers the single-cell gate. Three unverified accessions could reorder the shortlists: the McDole stage range, the Harland accession and the de Soysa accession. Nothing in this plan requires unlocking validation data.

## Task 3 gets a sign prior and a magnitude template, not a beta-catenin response

The Task 3 gap is 18.2 points, and 8.5 of it is severity_slope. That term rewards a response that correlates positively with the real one at a plausible magnitude, and a wrongly signed response scores far below 50, so the sign matters more than coverage. Every Ctnnb1 or Gata4 measurement is held out, including other alleles and phenocopies, which removes the direct evidence. The [GSE97761 TCF7L2 occupancy after beta-catenin stabilisation](https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE97761), the Ctnnb1 biotin-FLAG knock-in ChIP, and the knockout arm of [E-MTAB-10575](https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/biostudies-arrayexpress/E-MTAB-10575) are all RED. The most tempting analogue is a Wls deletion in the Mesp1 lineage ([bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.01.31.428968v1.full.pdf)). It matches our driver, tissue and pathway, and it removes Wnt secretion from the same cells, so I rate it FORBIDDEN as a phenocopy pending the organisers.

What remains is annotation. The MSigDB [canonical-Wnt GO set](https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb/mouse/geneset/GOBP_CANONICAL_WNT_SIGNALING_PATHWAY.html) is membership data rather than a measurement. Its partial mouse list includes Axin1, Axin2, Ctnnb1, Btrc and Dkk1, and the full count was not seen. The sign should come from the textbook direction of canonical targets, which fall when beta-catenin is lost, and not from loss-of-function lists. The [Nusse target table](https://web.stanford.edu/~rnusse/pathways/targets.html) is AMBER for up-direction rows only, because it dates from 2009 and mixes Xenopus, cell-line and loss-of-function sources. A motif-only [cisTarget](https://resources.aertslab.org/cistarget/) TCF/LEF ranking can weight panel genes by enhancer potential without any measurement, but its databases run from 1 to 100 GB and the licence is not stated.

Magnitude has to come from elsewhere. The one in-task knockout is Mab21l2 at E9.5, which the organisers provide for training, and it should be the first calibration. The closest stage-matched cardiac knockout with matched wild type is [de Soysa et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6719697), a Hand2-null single-cell study at E7.75, E8.25 and E9.25 with over 36,000 cells, and I could not find its accession. The closest design analogue is the [Sox17 Mesp1-Cre conditional at E8.5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6697751), GEO GSE125323. It shares the driver and the stage, but its platform is an Agilent microarray, so only log ratios are usable, and it reports 285 probes changed in endocardium and 358 in myocardium at a 5-fold cutoff. Sox17 is described as a beta-catenin antagonist, so its loss may raise Wnt rather than mimic Wnt loss. The knockout library rated this SAFE; I rate it RISKY.

## Embryo interpolation depends on the E7.25 and E8.0 brackets

The embryo gap of 2.3 points comes entirely from occupancy_dice, where we score 5.7 against 100, so the target is one coherent anatomy rather than more cell types. The rules remove everything strictly between E7.25 and E8.0, along with E7.5 and E7.75, and keep the two bracket stages themselves. The midpoint wording in the verified rules text is absorbed by this wider bracket, so it adds no further exclusion. E8.25 is allowed by the letter, since it sits closer to E8.0 than to the held-out E7.75. The E7.75 target lies 0.5 days from E7.25 and 0.25 days from E8.0, so both brackets carry the signal.

[Pijuan-Sala et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6522369) is the anchor. Its nine timepoints run from E6.5 to E8.5 across 116,312 cells, and the E7.25 and E8.0 timepoints can be taken from ArrayExpress E-MTAB-6967 once E7.5 and E7.75 are dropped. It is single-cell RNA only, so it supplies cell-state composition rather than positions. [Harland et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2025.116047) adds sequential-FISH spatial data at E7.25 on two-dimensional sections, with a 351-marker panel reported in the preprint. The preprint describes E6.5 and E7.5 embryos where the published article describes E7.25 and E7.5, so the stage needs confirming before use. Its accession has not been found.

The most useful new candidate is the 2025 Cell paper [Digital reconstruction of full embryos during early mouse organogenesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40920635/). It profiled serial sections from six embryos on the Stereo-seq platform and built three-dimensional digital embryos. The [BGI release](https://en.genomics.cn/scientificdiscoverie-7162.html) names E7.5, E7.75 and E8.0 and 104,343 cells. This is a spanning resource under the rules: the E7.5 and E7.75 embryos must come out per embryo, and the reconstruction must be rebuilt from the E8.0 embryos alone, not trimmed afterwards. The GEO record [GSE278603](https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE278603) lists 360 sections, where the abstract reports 285, so the accession needs checking against the paper's data statement. It is the only three-dimensional single-cell bracket candidate in the notes.

EMAP anatomy models look useful for occupancy, but their nominal stages cause trouble. TS10 is labelled 7.0 dpc with a 6.5 to 7.75 range, and TS12 is labelled 8.0 with a 7.5 to 8.75 range ([EMAP stage definitions](https://emouseatlas.org/emap/ema/theiler_stages/StageDefinition/stagedefinition.html)). The rules treat a dataset labelled with a Theiler stage that lands in the held-out window as held out, so both are RISKY until the organisers accept nominal labels. The [McDole 2018 in toto light-sheet dataset](https://janelia.org/lab/keller-lab/software/in-toto-imaging-and-reconstruction-of-post-implantation-mouse-development-at) would rank first if its stage list covers E7.25 and E8.0 at cell resolution. I could not verify that range or find an accession, although the [HHMI release](https://www.hhmi.org/node/1610) says the imaging data are public.

## Task 1 can use E14.5 bulk and E8.5 nuclei now

The data page makes Task 1 the only task with explicit permission for external single-cell data, and only E10.5 and E12.5 are held out. The window excludes anything after E9.5 up to and including E13.5, so E9.5 exactly is allowed and anything from E14.0 onward is allowed with disclosure. The late heart anchors are covered in `/scratch/user/virtual-embryo/reports/Late stage heart anchors.md` and are not repeated here. The Task 1 gap of 8.7 points has no per-term breakdown in the notes, so the ranking below is by evidence fit rather than by a named score term.

The most useful cleared source beyond those anchors is bulk. The [ENCODE E14.5 heart polyA RNA-seq experiment ENCSR727FHP](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR727FHP/) has two isogenic replicates and gives a whole-transcriptome heart reference two days after the target. The encodeproject page returned HTTP 403 to my fetch, so this is verified from a search summary only. The assay mismatch matters here. Our target is single-nucleus RNA, while the late heart sets Feulner and Ren are whole-cell scRNA. Qiu is the only late nuclei source with cardiac labels. Ren's intronic counts might approximate nuclei, but that is untested. ENCODE therefore calibrates expression levels at E14.5, and Qiu supplies nuclei.

The early nuclei sets matter less, because E8.5 and E9.5 are already released training stages. [GSE186069](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE186nnn/GSE186069/suppl/) holds 154,313 kept E8.5 nuclei with first and second heart field, splanchnic and endocardial labels, which adds early heart-lineage vocabulary. The [MOCA atlas](https://krishna.gs.washington.edu/documents/cao_nature_2019.pdf) contributes E9.5 nuclei only, and its E10.5 to E13.5 cells are RED. For gene-level priors, [Eurexpress](https://www.eurexpress.org/) gives image-based E14.5 whole-embryo in situ data for more than 18,000 genes, and [MGI GXD](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4534488) assays filtered to E9.5 exactly and E14.0 onward, with every mutant dropped, form a further prior. Eurexpress states no licence, so that needs confirming before use.

## One organiser message gates most single-cell sources

The data page limits external single-cell training to Task 1 ([Data](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/data)), while rules section 10 allows external data for every task ([Rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules)). Until that is settled, every single-cell source above is stage-safe but NOT CLEARED for Tasks 2 and 3. Bulk, annotation and Geo-seq-style spatial bins are arguably outside the single-cell sentence, but the organisers have not said so. The drafted question already covers the Task 2 and 3 conflict, gene-level annotations, Task 3 other-gene knockouts, phenocopy boundaries and per-setting held-out stages. Six questions are new:

1. Narrow reading: does "external public single-cell data" leave out bulk RNA-seq, ChIP, gene-level annotation and Stereo-seq spatial bins for Tasks 2 and 3? If so, those become usable now with disclosure.
2. Spanning reconstructions: may a three-dimensional reconstruction built from six embryos at E7.5, E7.75 and E8.0 be used if only the E8.0 embryos enter it, with the reconstruction re-run?
3. Label transfer: does using labels from an atlas that contains held-out stages count as use "through a model pre-trained on it"? Mittnenzweig's labels come from Pijuan-Sala, and Harland integrates several atlases.
4. Nominal Theiler labels: are EMAP TS10 (7.0) and TS12 (8.0) acceptable on their nominal label, when the rules treat a Theiler-labelled dataset in the window as held out?
5. Literature route: is a published narrative description of Ctnnb1 or Wnt loss in mesoderm, with no data, a route to held-out data? If yes, the Task 3 sign must come from annotation alone.
6. Task 3 wild type: is wild-type E8.25 to E8.5 data blocked, given that E8.75 is held out only for the knockout genotypes?

## Conclusion

For Task 3, the lever is the sign of the response, not coverage. With every beta-catenin measurement excluded, the useful resources are annotation and calibration, and the wrong-sign penalty makes restraint the right default. The embryo task depends on exact bracket stages, so an atlas is worth what survives from its E7.25 and E8.0 timepoints rather than its size. The E8.0-only subset of the Cell 2025 reconstruction is the strongest unverified three-dimensional candidate, and it needs the spanning-resource treatment before any use.

For Task 1, the gain beyond the heart anchors is a cleared bulk heart reference, which covers the level question, and the nuclei gap is one that the heart report's whole-cell sets cannot close. Qiu remains the only late nuclei source, which makes the Qiu cardiac-cluster work the critical path. The three unverified accessions could reorder the embryo and Task 3 shortlists, and each is cheap to check before the deadline.
