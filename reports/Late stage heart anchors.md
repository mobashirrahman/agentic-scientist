# Qiu anchors Task 1; heart awaits ruling

**Qiu et al. 2024 is the strongest late anchor for Task 1, and part of it is already on disk.** Its E14.0 to E14.75 embryos (about 1.0 million whole-embryo cells) and the E15 donors already in use sit after the protected window, so they are allowed once the source and stages are disclosed, and it is the only late single-nucleus source with cardiomyocyte labels that we found. For Task 2 heart, four public sources match the late stages the rules allow: Feulner 2024 (GEO GSE150817) and Ren 2023 (GEO GSE230531) heart single-cell sets at E14.5 and E16.5, whole-embryo Stereo-seq (MOSTA) at E14.5 and E15.5, and a heart Stereo-seq study in Nature Communications 2023 whose accession we have not found. **None of these is cleared for Task 2 yet**, because the Data page limits external single-cell training to Task 1 while Rules section 10 permits it for all tasks. Download the Qiu E14 to E17 cardiac clusters for Task 1 first, and stage Feulner (106 MB, wild type, heart only, no held-out stages) for Task 2, with no Task 2 training until the organisers reply. The Nature Communications accession is the most valuable open item, because it is the only late spatial heart candidate that matches both stage and neighbourhood need. The local harness also needs changes, since its heart check misses the held-out E8.5 and the excluded bracket. No late heart dataset carries 3D coordinates, and the organiser question should go out now, with the competition deadline on 2 December.

## Qiu supplies the only late snRNA heart signal for Task 1

Qiu et al. 2024 is sci-RNA-seq3 single-nucleus sequencing of whole embryos from E8 to birth ([Nature](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10901739/)). Its supplementary Table 5 labels a cardiomyocyte cluster with first-heart-field, second-heart-field, ventricular and atrial subtypes, plus endocardial cells (from an earlier pass over the Springer supplement). The day labels in supplementary Table 1 ([Springer spreadsheet](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-024-07069-w/MediaObjects/41586_2024_7069_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx)), which I parsed this session, cover **99 embryos**. Four embryos at E14.0 to E14.75 hold **about 1.0 million whole-embryo cells**, and a fifth E14.5 row reports zero cells. The late pool then runs through E15 (four embryos, 0.83 million cells, already staged), E16, E17, E18 and four P0 embryos. The E14.0 donors are the nearest allowed stage to the E12.5 target, but they sit only half a day past the window edge, so the organisers should confirm them before use.

The late cardiac signal we already stage is thin. The local `anchor_cardiomyocytes.h5ad` holds **380 cells** from embryo_57 to embryo_60, all Theiler stage 23, matching Table 1's E15.0 to E15.75. Of those cells, 339 are labelled "cardiac muscle cell" and 41 "splanchnic mesodermal cell", across 53,384 genes (all checked this session). Qiu is whole-embryo, so "heart region" in Task 1 means selecting cardiac clusters rather than dissected hearts. The two dissected-heart scRNA sets below fit that definition more closely, but neither is single-nucleus.

## Four heart sources match late stages, but none offers 3D

Feulner and Ren are dissociated single-cell sets with no coordinates, so they can anchor cell proportions, the cell-distribution failure in our first entry, but not neighbourhoods. Qiu's late donors are also allowed by letter for the heart board and give a composition reference of the same kind, but they fall under the same gate. MOSTA supplies whole-transcriptome **28,879-gene** Stereo-seq across sagittal sections ([STOmicsDB](https://db.cngb.org/stomics/datasets/STDS0000058/)), which supports neighbourhood and scale work. Heart is a small share of each whole-embryo section, though, and the dataset page lists six sections while a portal summary lists 53, so the section count needs checking. The Nature Communications 2023 conduction-system study is the best stage match for spatial heart data, with E14.5, E16.5 and P3 allowed and E10.5 and E12.5 removed, but its accession, counts and labels come from a search snippet, and the article page redirects to a login ([Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42658-9)). The cardiac Stereo-seq atlas ([STT0000062](https://db.cngb.org/stomics/project/STT0000062)) is heart-specific and spatial, with 330,857 bin50 bins over 22 sections and 14 cell types ([GigaScience](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11831923/)). Its stages run from E20 to P14, which is 7.5 days or more past E12.5, so it shows direction of maturation rather than matched timepoints.

The only 3D transcriptome we found is MOSTA3D, at E9.5 and E11.5 ([bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.08.17.608366.full.pdf)). E9.5 is usable by letter, E11.5 is forbidden, and access is unverified. A late 3D heart would need a new reconstruction, and sagittal sections from different embryos do not register into one volume. A Visium and Slide-seq embryonic and neonatal heart study attributed to Hu et al. ([aggregator](https://synapsesocial.com/papers/W7161294153)) could be the most useful spatial embryonic source, but its stages, which could include E10 to E13, and its accession are unidentified, so it is a lead rather than a candidate.

Several sources drop out. The Bruker CosMx mouse embryo data are an unpublished poster with no public accession, and E11 to E13 are forbidden in it ([poster](https://brukerspatialbiology.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AACR_2026_ProductDevelopment_MouseEmbryo_MusWTx_mpatrick_60x45.pdf)). The adult MERFISH heart has 128 genes, too few for a 500-gene panel ([abstract](https://bohrium.dp.tech/paper/arxiv/949051879873052678)). GEO GSE178636 covers E10.5 to E12.5, which is forbidden ([OmicsDI](https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE178636)). Early atlases such as Pijuan-Sala and the E8.5 spatial sets cannot supply late anchors, because E8.5 is held out for the heart board.

## One Data-page sentence gates every Task 2 source

Rules section 10 says external public data may be used "provided their licences permit it and their use is described in the method summary", and calls Task 1 use "expressly encouraged" ([Rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules)). The Data page then says both "You may train on external data, public or your own, and for Task 1 this is expressly encouraged" and, in the Task 1 description, "Task 1 is the only task where training on external public single-cell data is allowed, provided the source is disclosed with the submission" ([Data](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/data)). Both cannot hold for Task 2. The temporal task page repeats the Task 1 permission ([Temporal](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/tasks/temporal)), and the spatial page has no external-data rule. Until the organisers settle the conflict, every single-cell or spatial source is stage-allowed but not cleared for the heart board, while Task 1 use is cleared. Stereo-seq bins may fall outside "single-cell", but the conservative reading covers them. Task 3 falls under the same gate.

The stage rules for the heart board are as follows. The held-out heart stages are E8.5, E10.5 and E12.5. External heart data strictly between E8.25 and E8.75 is excluded. The window excludes everything after E9.5 up to and including E13.5, so E9.5 exactly is allowed and E13.5 exactly is not, and anything after E13.5 "may be used, provided the source and its stages are stated explicitly with the submission" ([Rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules)). Three corrections follow. Ren's E8.5 whole embryos, which the earlier notes treated as before the window, are held out for the heart board and usable only for Task 1. Every multi-stage resource (Qiu, Ren, Nature Communications, MOSTA) needs in-window cells removed before training, with the removal described in the method summary. Qiu's bin labels straddle the boundary, so filtering must use per-embryo days from Table 1, not bin names. Each submission must name Qiu with its DOI and CELLxGENE collection, the stages used, and the cells removed. No source checked so far is a Gata4 or Ctnnb1 perturbation, though the genotype checks for Qiu, Goodyer and Nature Communications are incomplete.

The rules invite a question before submission ("If a source sits near the boundary and you are unsure, ask before submitting"), and the organisers have said an advance answer is not a finding of misconduct. Suggested wording for the organisers: "The Data page says Task 1 is the only task where training on external public single-cell data is allowed, but Rules section 10 allows external public data for all tasks. Does the Data-page sentence restrict the Task 2 heart board? If it does, may we train it on wild-type heart single-cell and spatial data at E14.5 and E16.5 (Feulner et al. 2024, GEO GSE150817; Nature Communications 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-42658-9), with E8.5 and E10.5 to E13.5 cells removed and the removal described? Are held-out stages applied per setting, so that the heart-held-out E8.5 does not block embryo-setting data? Is a Qiu donor at E14.0 to E14.25 acceptable for Task 1, given that UNSW places Theiler stage 23 at E14.5 ([UNSW](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php/Mouse_Timeline_Detailed)) while the paper's day labels put our TS23 donors at E15.0 to E15.75?"

## Staged files and harness need repair before any heart anchor

The staged Task 1 files are compliant, but they show where the heart path would fail. The 14-donor calibration set is Theiler stage 12 (embryo_1 to embryo_12, E8.0 to E8.5 in Table 1) and stage 13 (embryo_13 and embryo_14, E8.75 to E9.5). The stage-13 cell count reconciles exactly to those two donors (141 + 998 = 1,139), which confirms the day mapping. This set is allowed for Task 1, where E8.5 and E9.5 are released stages. It cannot enter the heart board without per-embryo filtering, because the TS12 donors span the held-out E8.5 and the excluded bracket (E8.25, E8.75). The heart board currently receives no external data, since `data/external/heart_anchor` does not exist and `external_board` in `shinka_ve/harness.py` returns None. That is the right state.

Two code gaps need fixing before anyone creates the heart folder. `load_anchor` in `shinka_ve/harness.py` asserts only that each listed day is at most E9.5 or above E13.5 and that no perturbation is flagged, so a file labelled E8.5 would pass. The check also takes one day list per file, while a heart file would mix embryos, so the manifest needs per-cell day labels. The temporal loader in `src/v2/anchor.py` checks Theiler labels only (TS12, TS13, TS23), so it cannot see the TS23 day conflict described above. Smaller items: every cell in `calibration_cardiomyocytes.h5ad` carries the label "splanchnic mesodermal cell", so its cardiomyocyte identity rests on `qiu_cluster`, which I did not check, and the manifest's cap of 4,000 cells is exceeded by several files, including `anchor_mesoderm.h5ad` at 20,000 cells.

## Conclusion

The bottleneck for Task 2 is clearance and accession, not data. Every heart source that matches the late stages is allowed by letter, but the one candidate that would address the neighbourhood and scale failures, the Nature Communications heart Stereo-seq, has no verified accession, so the search and the organisers' reply both sit on the critical path. Cell proportions can move ahead on Feulner's 106 MB file, while the spatial anchor waits on both.

For Task 1 the more useful move is depth. Qiu's E14 to E17 donors, selected by per-embryo day, would replace a 339-cell cardiac anchor with a larger late set from a source already disclosed, and the Feulner and Ren scRNA sets then serve as an independent check on cardiac programmes rather than as snRNA substitutes. Whatever the organisers say, the harness must reject E8.5 and the open bracket for the heart board before a heart file exists, because the current check would let them through.
